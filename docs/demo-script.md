## 0–15 seconds: the decision

“Before a football match, everyone has a view. After the result, it is easy to forget why. Sober lets fans keep the call, the reason and the evidence they actually saw before kickoff.”

## 15–35 seconds: why Panta belongs here

“Panta provides the market question, current phase and available prices. Sober puts that market next to a football decision. Fan forecasts and market prices remain clearly separate.”

Show the Panta phase, timestamp and refresh control. If prices are unavailable, explain that state. Do not replace absent quotes with mock prices in a live screen.

## 35–60 seconds: make and retain a call

“I can save a free forecast with my own reason. Sober retains the fixture context and any Panta prices available at that moment. If I used an analyst snapshot, its version stays attached.”

On the live app, the demonstrator connects their own wallet and approves a sign-in message, then saves a forecast. On the practice app, say “This is a local practice record.” Show the saved card and expand its context. Do not say the opinion is an on-chain attestation.

## 60–80 seconds: return to the record

“My notebook brings those calls together. I can find an earlier reason, copy it or export the record. Once its recorded cutoff passes, I cannot rewrite that forecast.”

Open My notebook, search the saved reason, expand the captured context and show the export. Automatic result grading and after-match performance scores are not implemented.
